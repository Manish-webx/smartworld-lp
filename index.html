The Edition is a luxury residential development by Smartworld in Sector 66, Gurugram, offering 3.5 and 4.5 BHK residences across three twin high-rise towers.

The project is located in Sector 66, on Golf Course Extension Road, Gurugram — one of the city's most sought-after residential corridors.

Residences are offered in 3.5 BHK and 4.5 BHK configurations, with four layout types (A, A1, B and B1), each with a utility room.

The Edition comprises three high-rise twin towers, each rising 42+ floors.

Signature features include a private jacuzzi, a private deck and a rooftop infinity pool, set amid landscaped central greens and designed with leading architecture, landscape and interior consultants.

Amenities include a gymnasium, yoga pods, jogging track, beach park, kids' play area, skating park and courts for tennis, squash, badminton and basketball.

It is designed for families and discerning buyers seeking spacious, design-led homes with premium amenities in a well-connected Gurugram location.
